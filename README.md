## What is Flavoratory?
Have you ever questioned what makes specific ingredients achieve perfect flavor harmony while others completely clash? Have you ever wanted to explore the molecular foundations of culinary arts and map out the hidden rules of taste creation? 
If so, Flavoratory is the ultimate guide for your exploration!

Our mission is to translate the intuitive "art" of cooking into a transparent, data-driven "science" of flavor compatibility. To achieve this, our application walks you through a three-tier interactive framework, moving smoothly from microscopic chemistry to macroscopic global food habits.

The journey begins with The Science (Network Explorer): an interactive data dictionary enabling you to visually track how chosen ingredients align via shared volatile compound profiles, enhanced by a responsive magnifying lens for close inspection. From there, we transition to The Sociology (Cultural Maps): a global mapping workspace where you can investigate regional culinary data and evaluate the "Food Pairing Hypothesis" revealing why Western traditions favor heavy molecular overlap while Eastern cuisines intentionally celebrate contrasting combinations. Finally, you arrive at The Application (Pairing Oracle): a dynamic visualization turning you from a passive reader into an active recipe developer. Simply input a core ingredient, choose your preferred complexity level, and the system instantly charts mathematically sound Traditional or Radical pairings utilizing a unique half-circle network and real-time compound overlays.

Grab your favorite ingredients, fire up your browser, and click the link above to start experimenting in our flavor laboratory!

## Who was it created for?
Designed for anyone captivated by cooking, culinary arts, and food science!
Enthusiastic beginners can easily navigate the world of molecular gastronomy through our clean layout structures, while seasoned chefs and data researchers can evaluate advanced theories using our highly responsive graphs.

## Project Installation
To launch the website locally, follow these steps:

1. Clone this repository.
2. Change directories into the primary source folder `PG` if not inside it.
3. Launch a local web server (e.g., using Node's `http-server` or the Live Server extension if using vscode).
